Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 7, 2026

JobDirect subscriptions are managed through Apple App Store (iOS) and Google Play (Android). Refunds and cancellations are handled by the respective platform.

1. Subscription plans

JobDirect offers the following monthly subscription plans:

  • Free: No cost, no payment required
  • Pro: ₹599 per month
  • Elite: ₹999 per month

All subscriptions are billed monthly and renew automatically at the end of each billing period.

2. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time:

  • iOS: Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → JobDirect → Cancel
  • Android: Go to Google Play Store → Subscriptions → JobDirect → Cancel

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You will continue to have access to your paid features until the period ends. No further charges will be made after cancellation.

3. Refunds

As JobDirect is a digital subscription service with immediate access to all features upon payment, we generally do not offer refunds for the current billing period once a subscription has been charged.

However, refund requests may be considered in the following circumstances:

  • Duplicate charges due to a technical error
  • Charges after a confirmed cancellation
  • Service was completely inaccessible for a significant portion of the billing period due to our error

To request a refund, please contact us at jobdirect01@gmail.com within 7 days of the charge with your transaction details.

For iOS subscriptions, you may also request a refund directly through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

4. Free trial

If a free trial is offered, it will be clearly communicated before activation. After the trial period ends, your subscription will automatically convert to a paid plan unless cancelled before the trial expires.

5. Price changes

We reserve the right to change subscription pricing. Existing subscribers will receive at least 30 days notice before any price change takes effect.

6. Contact us

For any questions about refunds or cancellations, please contact us:
